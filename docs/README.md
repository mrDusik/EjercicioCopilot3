# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

This folder contains the complete OctoAcme project management process documentation. OctoAcme follows a structured lifecycle-based approach that emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, and clear ownership. The framework spans five phases: **Initiation**, **Planning**, **Execution**, **Release**, and **Closeout & Retrospective**. 

The methodology is built on core principles of customer-first prioritization, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety. Work is coordinated through clearly defined roles (Project Managers, Product Managers, and Developers), a structured team rhythm with daily standups and weekly syncs, and continuous risk management. Quality is enforced through automated testing, code review, and security scanning in CI, while retrospectives drive continuous improvement. This combination of clear processes, transparent communication, and structured quality gates enables OctoAcme teams to deliver reliable, maintainable software on time and within scope.

## Key Artifacts

Across the OctoAcme process, teams create and maintain these key artifacts:

- **Project One-pager** — Problem statement, goals, success metrics, stakeholders, and timeline (from Initiation)
- **Roadmap and Release Plan** — High-level sequencing of features and releases (from Planning)
- **Backlog with Acceptance Criteria** — Prioritized work items with clear Definition of Done (from Planning)
- **Risk Register** — Identified risks with impact, likelihood, owners, and mitigation plans (from Planning & Execution)
- **Project Board** — Visual tracking of work through Backlog → Ready → In Progress → In Review → QA → Done (from Execution)
- **Release Notes** — Summary of changes, migration steps, and known issues (from Release)
- **Retrospective Notes and Action Items** — Learnings and commitments to improvement (from Closeout)

## Communication Cadence

- **Daily:** 15-minute standups (focus: progress, blockers, dependencies)
- **Weekly:** PM + PdM sync; twice-weekly delivery standups
- **Sprint/Milestone:** Demos and reviews with stakeholders
- **Monthly:** Stakeholder updates on progress and metrics
- **Ad-hoc:** Risk escalations and incident communications
